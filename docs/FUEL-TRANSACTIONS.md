# Fuel transactions

Batch 9 can correct a completed-trip fuel record’s historical effective odometer/amount through an append-only adjustment. It does not change Vehicle.odometer or lower the trusted reviewed reading. See [CLOSED-TRIP-ADJUSTMENTS.md](CLOSED-TRIP-ADJUSTMENTS.md).

Fuel is an explicit structured subtype in immutable `expense_revisions`, with liters, price_per_liter and optional odometer, plus vendor/station, reference/receipt number, notes and occurrence time. Root identity binds it to the trip's assigned tenant vehicle/driver. Database constraints reject inconsistent fuel amounts or fuel fields on other categories.

Liters: >0, <=10,000, maximum three decimal places. Price per liter: >0, <=10,000 PHP, maximum four decimal places. Server computes liters × price using Decimal and ROUND_HALF_UP to two places. The result must be >0 and <=10,000,000 PHP. Client amount must be omitted/null for Fuel. Example: 52.35 × 61.75 = 3232.61 PHP. 1 × 0.005 = 0.01 PHP. PostgreSQL also checks amount equals rounded product.

Odometer is optional when unavailable, nonnegative, <=10,000,000 km, maximum one decimal place. If supplied, it cannot be lower than the greater of Vehicle.odometer and the highest previously reviewed fuel odometer for that vehicle, retained separately in vehicles.reviewed_fuel_odometer. Validation runs on submission, correction and review under the existing organization lock. Backdated entries receive no automatic exception. Vehicle.odometer is never changed by expense input. A review trigger monotonically advances the separate internal reviewed_fuel_odometer; unreviewed inputs cannot advance it. This scalar remains enforceable across driver reassignment without disclosing the previous driver's expense history. Corrections use the expense's original vehicle snapshot. Owners must resolve incorrect master readings through their existing authorized master-data workflow; no silent override is added. Voiding/correcting an expense does not decrease this historical reviewed reading; correcting an erroneously high reviewed reading requires a future administrative workflow.

Vehicle detail exposes paginated actual fuel records: date, trip, driver, liters, price, total, odometer and state. No mileage/fuel-economy claim is made. Corrections retain the original fuel revision, and void removes its current amount from operational totals without deletion. A correction changing category is visible in the trip's revision history; current Fuel history uses current category.

Fuel and general expense audit events are both recorded for fuel mutations. Evidence remains optional/private and uses the same validation as delivery images. Detailed API/currency semantics are in TRIP-EXPENSES.md; verification outcomes are in BATCH-8-REPORT.md.
